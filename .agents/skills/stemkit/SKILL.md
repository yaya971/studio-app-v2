# 🎛️ Stemkit — Neural Audio Stem Separator & Mini-DAW

Located in `tools/stemkit/`.

## Capabilities
- **HTDemucs Neural Engine**: State-of-the-art stem separation into Vocals, Drums, Bass, Other, Guitar, and Piano.
- **Apple Silicon MPS Hardware Acceleration**: Optimized for M1/M2/M3/M4 GPUs with float32/fp16 PyTorch acceleration.
- **Synced Multi-track Web Audio Player**: Web Audio API mixer with solo/mute, volume faders, waveform visualizations, and pitch/speed control.
- **Stem Export**: High-fidelity WAV and FLAC stem exports for music production, remixing, sampling, and karaoke isolation.
