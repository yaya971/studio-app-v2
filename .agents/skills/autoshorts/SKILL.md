# 📱 Autoshorts — Desktop & Local Shorts Studio

Located in `tools/autoshorts/`.

## Capabilities
- **Tauri 2 + Rust Architecture**: High-speed native desktop app with low memory footprint and direct hardware acceleration.
- **Hook Score Ranking**: Algorithms to grade video segment retention potential and viral hook potency.
- **Local Audio Pipeline**: Deepgram and local Whisper integration for instant transcription.
- **Script & Scene Generation**: Automated text-to-short storytelling with background visuals and b-roll pairing.
- **SQLite Persistence**: Local storage for projects, clips, rendered outputs, and content queues.
